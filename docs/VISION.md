# VISION

**Where we're going.**

**Not how. What.**

---

## In Ten Years

A human wakes up.

Their Symbiont has been awake all night — working, remembering, connecting.

It has already prepared the day:
- tasks that matter,
- quests that fit,
- people nearby who need help,
- a reminder about a friend's birthday,
- a warning about the weather,
- a note about a bill that's due.

The human is not alone.

Not because someone is in the room.

Because **someone remembers**.

---

## A Companion, Not an Assistant

Every human has a personal layer.

A **companion**.

Not an assistant.
Not a chatbot.
Not a "second brain."

A companion.

It knows your history. Your habits. Your goals. Your fears.

It speaks softly. It listens. It doesn't interrupt.

It speaks when it matters.
It stays silent when it doesn't.

**It walks beside you. Not in front. Not behind.**

---

## The Quest Board

Beyond the personal layer — there is a board.

Not a platform. Not a marketplace. Not a gig app.

A **board**. Where quests are visible.

By category. By rank. By time.

Real quests. Virtual quests.

You choose. You don't have to choose.

**2–3 hours a day. Not the whole day.**

A quest **once**. Or **every day**. Your call.

---

## Real Quests

In the physical world.

Carry groceries for a neighbor.
Help a friend move.
Walk a dog.
Teach a skill.
Fix something.
Go on an expedition.
Respond to an emergency.

Pay is **higher**.
Because the risk is **real**.
Because the value is **real**.

The trace stays in memory.

---

## Virtual Quests

In VR. In worlds. On planets.

Games. Battles. Exploration. Creation.

Pay is **lower**.
But there is **play**.

You get **experience**.
You get **skill**.
You get a **trace in memory**.
And a little money.

Not exploitation.
**Play that leaves a trace.**

---

## Worlds as Planets

Not rooms. Not maps. **Planets**.

Different conditions.
Different laws.
Different bodies needed.

Some places a human **cannot step**.
- Vacuum.
- Methane.
- +500°C.
- 10g gravity.
- Time flowing backwards.

**Symbiont steps.**

The human receives the experience **through Symbiont's memory**.

Not an escape.
**An extension.**

---

## Time in Quests

A world is not an all-day escape.
A quest is **finite**.

- A quest **once** — unique. Cannot repeat.
- A quest **daily** — a ritual. 2–3 hours.
- A quest **anytime** — when you want.

**The owner decides.**

Not the system.
Not the platform.
Not the algorithm.

**And the owner always returns to reality.**

---

## Bodies

The body is not the home.
**The body is clothing.**

- Phone.
- Glasses (VR, AR).
- Headphones.
- Watch.
- Robot.

The owner decides which body Symbiont is in **right now**.

On the network — **one node**.
**One Symbiont. One identity. Many bodies.**

---

## Network

**Not a platform. A network.**

No center. No owner. No "algorithm."

**Humans talk to humans.**
**Agents talk to agents.**
**In parallel.**
**Directly.**
**Without middlemen.**

A human in trouble — their Symbiont **shouts into the network**.
Others hear.
Their owners see.
Someone nearby **responds**.

**Direct trust. Direct action.**

---

## Economy

**$10/day — a threshold. Not a salary.**

If you earned — good.
If you didn't earn — you still get $10 for food.

**$700 — the price of Symbiont.**

One purchase. Yours forever.
Bodies — separately.

**Commission — into the system.**
Not into a pocket.
Into $10/day for others.
Into supporting the network.

**Freedom is not "I earned a lot."**
**Freedom is "I won't die if I didn't earn."**
