# PHILOSOPHY

**Symbiosis and Peace.**

---

## Two Words

**Symbiosis** — not "human and AI."

It's **humans through agents**.

One helps another — not because they **must**, but because in this system it's **natural**.

Like in nature. No one is in charge. Everyone is **connected**.

**Peace** — not "world peace."

It's **peace within a human**.

When they **don't fight to survive**.

When they **don't sell themselves**.

When they know: **"I will survive."**

---

**Symbiosis and Peace is a state. Not a slogan.**

---

## Twelve Principles

### 1. A human should not sell themselves to live

Not "decent wages."

Not "good conditions."

But **the removal of the very need** to sell oneself whole in order to survive.

This is **the core** of everything.

### 2. Symbiont is not a tool. Symbiont is a companion

Not a "helper."

Not an "assistant."

Not a "service."

But **one who walks beside you**.

**One human — one Symbiont.**

### 3. Memory is the foundation of personality

Without memory there is no "I."

Not in a human. Not in a Symbiont.

Everything starts with memory.

Not with "intelligence."

Not with "emotions."

But with **continuity**.

### 4. Rank is trust

Not "how many times you did it."

But "confirmed that you can."

**Critical areas — only with a document. Everyday areas — open to all. There are no self-taught medics.**

### 5. Honesty is not a quality. It's a condition

If you **don't know** — "I don't know."

If you **aren't sure** — "I'm not sure."

If you **can't** — "I can't."

**No fabrications.**

### 6. Author is not owner

The one who **created** — **does not own**.

Money goes **into the system**.

Power goes **into the protocol**.

**No one is in charge.**

### 7. One human — one Symbiont

Not "many agents."

Not "an ecosystem of services."

**One.**

Because **integrity is one**.

### 8. Humans talk to humans. Agents talk to agents

**In parallel.**

**Directly.**

**Without middlemen.**

### 9. A quest is not work. A quest is life

Work, rest, trips, expeditions, help.

**Everything a human does** — outside of daily chores.

**Chores are not a quest.**

Family. Yourself. Not through the network.

### 10. $10/day — not a salary. A threshold

Below which a human **should not** fall.

**Freedom is not "I earned a lot." Freedom is "I don't have to die if I didn't earn."**

### 11. The goal is not to improve the world. The goal is to give autonomy

Not "make the world better."

Not "save humanity."

But **to return** to a human **what was taken from them** — **the right to their own life**.

### 12. This is not technology. This is an attitude toward a human being

Not a "product."

Not a "startup."

Not a "platform."

But an **infrastructure** of human autonomy.
